Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 10, 2026

ANGAGSU Game Studio ("we", "us", or "our") develops mobile games, including Hayguard: Rise of Highland, Fantasy Forest, and Kitchen Chaos (the "Games"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard information when you download, install, or play our Games. By using our Games, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following categories of information:

  • Device Information: device model, operating system and version, unique device identifiers (such as advertising ID / IDFA / GAID), language settings, and mobile network information.
  • Gameplay Data: in-game progress, scores, achievements, session length, and settings, which may be stored locally or synced via a linked account (e.g., Google Play Games Services or Game Center).
  • Usage & Analytics Data: app interactions, crash reports, performance data, and general engagement metrics.
  • Purchase Information: if a Game offers in-app purchases, transaction data is processed by the applicable app store (Google Play or Apple App Store); we do not receive or store your payment card details.
  • Communications: information you voluntarily provide when you contact us for support or feedback, such as your email address and message content.

2. How We Use Information

  • To operate, maintain, and improve our Games and their features.
  • To save your progress and enable cloud sync or leaderboards where applicable.
  • To diagnose technical issues and fix bugs through crash and performance reporting.
  • To display advertising (where applicable) and measure ad performance.
  • To process in-app purchases through the relevant app store.
  • To respond to support requests and communicate important updates.
  • To comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms.

3. Third-Party Services

Our Games may integrate third-party services that collect information used to identify you or your device, including but not limited to:

  • Google Play Services / Apple Game Center — for achievements, leaderboards, and cloud saves.
  • Google Analytics / Firebase — for app usage analytics and crash reporting.
  • Google AdMob (or similar ad networks) — for displaying in-game advertisements.
  • Google Play Billing / Apple In-App Purchase — for processing in-app purchases.

These third parties have their own privacy policies governing how they handle information. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of Google and Apple for more details on their data practices.

4. Children's Privacy

Our Games are not directed at children under the age of 13 (or the applicable minimum age in your jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If any of our Games are designed for a general audience that may include children, we limit data collection to what is strictly necessary for the Game to function and rely on age-appropriate advertising settings and parental controls provided by app store platforms. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can promptly remove it.

5. Data Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with:

  • Service providers who help us operate our Games (analytics, hosting, advertising networks).
  • App store platforms (Google Play, Apple App Store) for purchase processing and account services.
  • Authorities, when required by law, legal process, or to protect our rights and the safety of our users.
  • A successor entity in the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets.

6. Data Retention & Security

We retain information only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this policy, or as required by law. We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information; however, no method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Your Rights & Choices

Depending on your location, you may have rights regarding your personal information, including the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict the use of your data, and the right to opt out of certain data processing (such as personalized advertising).

  • You can reset your advertising identifier or limit ad tracking in your device settings.
  • You can manage app permissions (e.g., notifications) through your device's settings menu.
  • You can request access to or deletion of your data by contacting us using the details below.

8. International Data Transfers

Our Games are developed in Armenia, and information we collect may be processed and stored in Armenia or other countries where our service providers operate. By using our Games, you consent to the transfer of information to countries that may have different data protection laws than your country of residence.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for legal, operational, or regulatory reasons. We will post the updated policy on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date. Continued use of our Games after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
